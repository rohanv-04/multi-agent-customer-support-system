# NovaCart Warranty Policy

## 1. Warranty Coverage
- All electronics, appliances, and hardware sold by NovaCart carry a 1-year limited manufacturer warranty from date of purchase.
- Covers manufacturing defects, component failure, and firmware malfunction under normal household usage.
- Does not cover accidental drops, liquid immersion, unauthorized tampering, or cosmetic wear and tear.

## 2. Warranty Claims Process
- To initiate a warranty claim, provide the original Order ID, serial number, and photo or video documentation of the failure.
- Replacement units or repair credits are authorized within 48 business hours.
