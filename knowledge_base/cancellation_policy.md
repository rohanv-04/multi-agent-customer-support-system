# NovaCart Cancellation Policy

## 1. Order Cancellation Window
- Orders may be self-cancelled by the customer or by an agent within 60 minutes of order placement if the order status is "Processing".
- Once an order transitions to "Shipped" or "In Transit", it cannot be cancelled. The customer must wait for package receipt and initiate a standard return.

## 2. Cancellation Refunds
- Cancelled orders trigger an immediate authorization reversal or direct refund to the payment card within 1-3 business days.
