# NovaCart Refund Policy

## 1. Overview
NovaCart strives for complete customer satisfaction. If you are not satisfied with your purchase, or if an issue occurs with delivery, our refund policy outlines the terms and conditions under which refunds are approved and processed.

## 2. Standard 30-Day Return Window
- Customers are eligible for a full refund on unopened and undamaged products returned within 30 days of the recorded delivery date.
- Items must include all original packaging, manuals, accessories, and tags.
- Perishable items, customized products, and opened software/digital downloads are non-refundable unless defective upon arrival.

## 3. Shipping Delays and Delivery Failures
- **Severe Delay Threshold**: If an order status is marked as "Delayed" and the expected delivery date has lapsed by more than **3 business days** without updated carrier movement, the customer is automatically eligible for an immediate full refund or a free expedited replacement upon request.
- If an order has been marked as "Delayed" for 3 or fewer days, customer support will issue a carrier tracking check and a $10 courtesy credit, while monitoring shipment progress.
- Once a refund for a delayed shipment is processed, if the package eventually arrives, the customer may keep the item as courtesy or return it with a complimentary prepaid return label.

## 4. Refund Processing Time and Methods
- Approved refunds are credited directly to the original payment method (Credit Card, Debit Card, PayPal, or NovaPay Balance).
- Digital refunds are processed instantly in our system (generating a unique Refund ID `REF-xxxxx`).
- Depending on the customer's financial institution, funds appear on the account statement within 3 to 5 business days.

## 5. Automated Agentic Refund Rules
- Autonomous AI support agents are authorized to automatically approve and execute refunds up to **$500.00** per order, provided that the order meets the severe delay criteria (>3 business days overdue) or verified product damage.
- Refund requests exceeding $500.00 or with conflicting order states must be routed to the Human Escalation Desk.
