# NovaCart Shipping Policy

## 1. Shipping Methods and Delivery Timelines
- **Standard Shipping**: 3–5 business days. Free on all orders over $35.00.
- **Expedited Shipping**: 2 business days guaranteed. $9.99 flat rate.
- **Next-Day Priority**: 1 business day for orders placed before 1:00 PM EST. $19.99 flat rate.

## 2. Order Tracking
- All packages are assigned an active tracking number via FedEx, UPS, or NovaCart Express Logistics.
- Tracking information updates within 24 hours of package dispatch from the fulfillment center.

## 3. Delays and Exceptions
- Severe weather, transit strikes, and customs inspection may induce transit delays.
- If delivery takes longer than 3 business days beyond the estimated date, an automated delay status is triggered in the customer order ledger, enabling customer service agents to initiate immediate refunds or replacements.
